title: "Vision and Intelligent Unmanned Systems"

We develop and benchmark lightweight computer vision models for real-time deployment on resource-constrained hardware. Our work evaluates compact YOLO architectures for small-target recognition in cluttered natural environments as well as thermal infrared imagery.

On the systems side, we integrate onboard object detection with GPS waypoint navigation on embedded platforms such as the Raspberry Pi 4 to build autonomous search-and-rescue drones. We also apply vision-based motion tracking to biomedical diagnostics, including smartphone-assisted quantification of hand movement disorders.
