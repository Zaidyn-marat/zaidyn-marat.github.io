We apply complex network theory and statistical physics to the design of self-organizing wireless infrastructures. For wireless mesh and software-defined networks, we develop routing metrics, box-covering algorithms, and gateway placement strategies based on information entropy and fractal dimension, validating protocol performance in both NS-3 simulations and Raspberry Pi hardware testbeds.

Our recent work extends to unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) communications and next-generation physical-layer architectures. This includes metaheuristic node placement, energy-efficient federated learning under location uncertainty, covert communications, and joint beamforming and power allocation for RIS-assisted OTFS-NOMA and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems.
