A large-scale additive manufacturing system with a 2-meter build envelope for rapid prototyping of full-size mechanical structures and custom enclosures.

In our unmanned systems and robotics workflows, the printer is used to fabricate lightweight airframe components, gimbal and camera mounts, and ruggedized housings for field-deployed sensor nodes.
