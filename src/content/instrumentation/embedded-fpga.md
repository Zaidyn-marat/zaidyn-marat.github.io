A collection of single-board computers, microcontrollers, and programmable logic hardware for physical-layer communication experiments and onboard robotics control.

Our testbeds include multiple Raspberry Pi units for real-time edge inference and wireless mesh routing validation, ESP32 and Arduino Nano boards for low-power distributed sensor networks and STEM workshops, and a Blackboard FPGA platform for hardware-level digital signal processing and logic design.
